# Marketing Executive

**Company:** Corallia Living Ltd (fictional lifestyle and home-goods retailer, 14 stores across Mauritius)
**Location:** Port Louis, Mauritius (hybrid: 3 days in office)
**Reports to:** Head of Marketing
**Salary band:** MUR 45,000 to 60,000 per month
**Contract:** Permanent, full-time

## About the role

Corallia Living is growing its online store and wants to reach younger customers. We are looking for a Marketing Executive to plan, run and measure our digital campaigns across social media, search and email. You will work closely with the e-commerce, store and design teams.

## Key responsibilities

- Plan and publish content across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn
- Run and optimise paid campaigns on Meta Ads and Google Ads with a monthly budget of about MUR 250,000
- Track performance in Google Analytics 4 and report results every month
- Write clear, on-brand copy in English and French
- Coordinate seasonal campaigns and in-store events with the store teams
- Manage the email newsletter and customer segments

## Must-have requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communication, Business or a related field
- At least 3 years of experience in digital marketing
- Hands-on experience managing social media channels (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or LinkedIn)
- Experience running paid advertising campaigns (Google Ads or Meta Ads)
- Ability to measure campaign performance with analytics tools such as Google Analytics 4
- Strong copywriting and content creation skills
- Fluent in English and French

## Nice-to-have

- SEO knowledge
- Email marketing experience (Mailchimp, Brevo or HubSpot)
- Design tools such as Canva or Adobe Creative Suite
- Experience in retail, e-commerce or FMCG
- Marketing budget management
- Event marketing experience
- Speaks Mauritian Creole
