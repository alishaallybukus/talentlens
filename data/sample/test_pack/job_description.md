# Job Description: Junior Software Developer

**Company:** TechNova Solutions Ltd
**Location:** Mauritius
**Employment Type:** Full-time
**Experience Level:** Entry-level (0–2 years)

## About the Role

TechNova Solutions Ltd is seeking a motivated Junior Software Developer to join our growing development team. The successful candidate will assist in designing, developing, testing, and maintaining web applications while working alongside experienced developers.

This position is ideal for someone with a strong foundation in programming, a willingness to learn new technologies, and an interest in building reliable, user-friendly software solutions.

## Key Responsibilities

* Develop and maintain web applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks.
* Write clean, readable, and maintainable code.
* Collaborate with designers, developers, and project managers.
* Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve software bugs.
* Participate in code reviews and contribute to team discussions.
* Test applications to ensure functionality, performance, and usability.
* Work with REST APIs and databases to support application features.
* Document technical processes and development activities.

## Required Qualifications

* A degree, diploma, or equivalent qualification in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
* Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
* Familiarity with at least one programming language, such as Python, Java, or JavaScript.
* Understanding of fundamental programming concepts, including variables, functions, loops, and object-oriented programming.
* Basic knowledge of databases and SQL.
* Familiarity with Git or another version control system.
* Good problem-solving and analytical skills.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills.

## Preferred Qualifications

* Experience with React, Vue.js, or another front-end framework.
* Familiarity with Node.js or another back-end technology.
* Experience building personal, academic, or professional software projects.
* Knowledge of API integration and basic software testing.
* Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.

## Essential Soft Skills

* Willingness to learn and accept constructive feedback.
* Ability to work independently and collaboratively.
* Attention to detail.
* Effective time management and organisational skills.
* Adaptability when working with new technologies.
* A proactive approach to solving problems.

## What We Offer

* Mentorship from experienced software developers.
* Opportunities for professional development and training.
* A collaborative and supportive working environment.
* Opportunities to contribute to real-world software projects.
* Career progression opportunities within the company.

## Application Requirements

Applicants should submit an up-to-date CV outlining their education, technical skills, relevant projects, work experience, and professional qualifications. Candidates with limited work experience are encouraged to highlight academic projects, personal projects, internships, and transferable skills.
